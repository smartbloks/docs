AIWA supports multi-person workflows at every stage of a project — invite team members to collaborate with role-based access, and invite clients to review and comment on builds without exposing the editor or other projects in your workspace. Whether you're a solo builder sharing a preview or an agency managing a team of fifteen, AIWA gives you the controls you need.

## Team Seats and Roles

The number of seats and available roles depends on your plan:

| Plan | Seats | Roles available |
|---|---|---|
| **Launchpad** | 1 (individual) | — |
| **Solo** | 1 | — |
| **Agency** | Up to 15 team seats | Admin, Member, Viewer, Client |
| **Enterprise** | Unlimited seats + clients | Admin, Member, Viewer, Client |

**Role definitions:**

- **Admin** — Full access to the workspace: can build, edit, deploy, manage billing, and invite members
- **Member** — Can build and edit projects they have been added to; cannot manage billing or workspace settings
- **Viewer** — Read-only access; can view projects and previews but cannot make changes
- **Client** — Can review a specific project and leave comments; cannot access the editor or other projects

## Inviting a Team Member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your workspace settings">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner of the AIWA dashboard, then select **Workspace Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Team tab">
    Select the **Team** tab from the settings sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    Click **Invite Member** and enter the email address of the person you want to invite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select their role">
    Choose their role from the dropdown: **Admin**, **Member**, or **Viewer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. The person receives an email invitation with a link to join your workspace. Once they accept, they appear in your team list and can access the projects assigned to them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Client Review Portal

<Info>
  The Client Review Portal is an Agency and Enterprise feature.
</Info>

The Client Review Portal lets you invite clients to review a specific project without giving them access to the editor, your other projects, or any workspace settings. Clients see only the live preview for the project you share with them, and they can leave comments on specific sections directly in the browser.

- Invite clients to review a single project without exposing the editor or other workspace content
- Clients can view the live preview and leave comments tied to specific sections of the page
- Client review links support three visibility levels: **Public**, **Unlisted**, or **Private**
- **To invite a client:** open the project → click **Share** → select **Invite Client** → enter the client's email address

## Visibility Controls

<Info>
  Visibility controls are available on Agency and Enterprise plans.
</Info>

Every AIWA project can be set to one of three visibility levels. You can change the visibility at any time from the **Share** menu in the editor.

| Visibility | Who can access |
|---|---|
| **Public** | Anyone with the link can view the live preview — no sign-in required |
| **Unlisted** | Accessible by direct URL but not publicly listed or discoverable |
| **Private** | Only invited team members and clients can view the project |

<Note>
  Client review and visibility controls are Agency and Enterprise features. Solo plan projects are private by default and cannot be shared with external clients through the review portal.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use the **Unlisted** visibility setting when you want to share a preview with stakeholders for feedback before going public. The preview is accessible to anyone you send the link to, but it won't appear in any public listings or search results.
</Tip>
