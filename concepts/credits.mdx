Credits are the unit of usage in AIWA. Every time you generate a project, build a new feature, or trigger a significant update, AIWA draws from your monthly credit balance to power the AI work behind the scenes. Credit usage scales with two things: the complexity of what you're building, and the AI model you choose. Larger, more intricate applications cost more credits to generate than simple ones, and more capable models — the ones that reason harder and produce more sophisticated output — carry a higher credit cost than their faster counterparts.

## What Consumes Credits

Not everything you do in AIWA draws from your credits. Here is what does and what doesn't:

**Actions that consume credits:**
- Generating or regenerating a project from a prompt
- Adding major new features to an existing application
- Running the AI Testing Agent on a build
- Making significant architectural changes to your app
- Mobile export — generating a signed APK (Android) or IPA (iOS) for app store submission

**Actions that do not consume credits:**
- Browsing your project dashboard
- Viewing live previews of your application
- Reading your version history
- Reviewing agent briefs and suggestions before approving them

## Model Credit Costs

You choose which AI model powers each build. Different models carry different credit costs based on their capability and compute requirements.

| Model | Best For | Relative Credit Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Claude Sonnet | Fast iterations, UI changes | Standard |
| Claude Opus | Complex logic, architecture | Higher |
| GPT-4o | Alternative general use | Standard |
| Gemini 2.5 Pro | Large context, complex apps | Higher |

When in doubt, start with Claude Sonnet for speed and switch to Claude Opus or Gemini 2.5 Pro when you need deeper reasoning on a complex data model or tricky backend logic.

## Credit Allowances by Plan

Your monthly credit balance depends on your plan. Unused credits reset at the start of each billing period and do not roll over to the following month.

| Plan | Monthly Credits | Approx. Full Apps |
|---|---|---|
| Launchpad (Free) | 45 | 1 small app |
| Solo ($25.99/mo annual) | 130 | ~2 full apps |
| Agency ($149.99/mo annual) | 790 | ~15 full apps |
| Enterprise | Custom | Volume |

The "approx. full apps" figure assumes mid-complexity applications built with Claude Sonnet. Larger apps, complex SaaS products, or builds using higher-cost models will consume more credits per project.

## Managing Your Credits

You can check your remaining credit balance at any time from the dashboard — look for the credit indicator in the top navigation or your account settings. When your balance runs low, you'll see a warning before you trigger any credit-consuming action.

A few things worth knowing:

- **Unused credits do not roll over.** Your balance resets at the start of each billing cycle.
- **Upgrades are prorated automatically.** If you upgrade mid-cycle, Stripe calculates the difference and applies a credit to your next invoice — you only pay for what you use.
- **Enterprise plans carry custom volume.** If you're building at scale or need predictable credit volumes for client work, [contact the AIWA team](https://app.aiwa.codes/enterprise) to discuss a custom arrangement.

<Tip>
  Start with the Launchpad free plan — 45 credits every month, no credit card required. It's enough to build and publish your first small app and get a feel for how AIWA works before committing to a paid plan.
</Tip>
