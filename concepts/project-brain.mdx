Most AI builders have a short memory. You describe your app, they generate code — but when you come back three months later to add a feature, they've forgotten everything: your data model, your component structure, the reasons you made certain decisions. The result is code that fights your existing architecture instead of fitting it, and features that quietly break the ones you already shipped. Project Brain™ is AIWA's answer to that problem. It maintains a living, structured record of every decision made about your project — from day one through every update — so your AI always knows exactly what it built and why.

## What Project Brain™ Remembers

Project Brain™ captures and retains the full context of your product as it grows:

- **Architecture decisions and rationale** — the structural choices that shaped your app and the thinking behind them
- **Database schema and data model choices** — every table, relationship, and constraint, kept in sync with your current build
- **Component relationships and dependencies** — which parts of your frontend and backend rely on each other
- **Integration configurations** — which third-party services are connected, how they're wired in, and what credentials or patterns are used
- **Version history and what changed in each iteration** — a timestamped record of every significant update so you can trace when and why anything changed
- **User flows and feature requirements** — the goals each feature was designed to serve, not just the code that implements them

## Why It Matters

Without persistent context, every edit is a risk. Describe a new feature to an AI that doesn't remember your project, and it generates code in a vacuum — code that may duplicate logic, contradict existing patterns, or silently break flows that were already working. The longer a project runs, the worse this gets.

With Project Brain™, AIWA carries the full weight of your product's history into every conversation. Adding a payment flow six months after launch is no different from adding it on day one — AIWA still knows your auth model, your database schema, and the conventions already established in your codebase. Nothing is forgotten, and nothing is improvised without context.

## Editing With Confidence

Every time you describe a change, AIWA consults your project memory before generating a single line of code. It checks what already exists, what depends on it, and what constraints are in place. New features are shaped to fit your existing architecture — extending it cleanly rather than working around it.

This means you can describe changes in plain English at any point in your product's life, without needing to re-explain your stack, re-document your data model, or worry that AIWA will overwrite something it shouldn't. The context is always there. The edits are always informed.
