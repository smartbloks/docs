AIWA doesn't hand you a code generator and wish you luck. When you build with AIWA, you get a team of specialised AI agents — each assigned to a distinct part of your product's lifecycle. One plans your architecture. One writes the code. One tests every build before it ships. And after launch, others watch your traffic, your conversions, and your revenue, surfacing problems and proposing fixes on an ongoing basis. This is what a real product team does. AIWA does it with AI, at a fraction of the cost and a fraction of the time.

## How Agents Work Together

Each agent owns a distinct phase of your product's life, and they hand off to each other cleanly:

1. **The AI Product Manager plans first.** Before a line of code is written, it maps your data model, user flows, component structure, and integration requirements.
2. **The AI Builder generates the application.** Full-stack code — frontend, backend, database, auth — produced together so everything connects properly from the start.
3. **The AI Testing Agent validates every build.** Nothing ships until it's been tested for layout, logic, auth, and mobile rendering. Regressions are caught before your users ever see them.
4. **Post-launch agents take over.** The SEO, CRO, and Revenue Agents begin monitoring your product in production. Each one watches a different dimension of performance and surfaces actionable insights on an ongoing basis.

When an agent spots something — a traffic drop, a conversion bottleneck, a churn signal — it sends you a brief with its analysis and a proposed fix. You review it, approve what makes sense, and AIWA ships the change.

## You're Always in Control

No agent makes changes to your product without your explicit approval. You receive briefs, review the suggested changes, and decide what gets implemented. AIWA handles the execution — you make the calls. This means you get the leverage of a full product team without giving up oversight of your own product.
