This guide walks you through everything you need to go from zero to a live, deployed application with AIWA. By the end, you'll have a free account, a running project, and a real URL you can share with anyone — no credit card, no code, and no prior setup required.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Create Your Free Account">
    Go to [app.aiwa.codes](https://app.aiwa.codes) and sign up for a free account. No credit card is required. Every new account starts with **45 free credits**, which is enough to generate and deploy a complete application right away.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start a New Project">
    Once you're on your dashboard, click **New Project**. You'll be prompted to choose your builder type based on what you want to create:

    - **Website** — marketing sites, landing pages, portfolios, and content-driven sites
    - **App** — full-stack web applications with databases, authentication, and business logic
    - **SaaS** — multi-tenant products with subscription billing and user management
    - **CRM** — custom customer relationship management tools built around your sales process

    Select the type that best matches your goal. You can always expand your project later.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe What You Want to Build">
    In the chat prompt, describe your idea in plain English. Tell AIWA who the product is for, what it does, and how users move through it. You don't need wireframes, technical specifications, or a design file — just write as you would explain it to a colleague.

    **Example:** *"Build a project management tool for small agencies. Teams can create projects, assign tasks to team members, set deadlines, and track progress with a Kanban board. Users log in with email and password."*

    The more context you provide, the more precisely AIWA can plan and build for your specific use case.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the AI Architecture Plan">
    Before writing a single line of code, AIWA's **AI Product Manager** reads your description and maps out the full scope of your project — user flows, data model, component structure, authentication approach, and any integration requirements. You'll see this plan before building begins.

    Review it, ask follow-up questions, or request adjustments. This is your chance to shape the architecture before it becomes code.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build and Preview">
    Click **Build** to kick off generation. AIWA's multi-model AI engine produces your complete full-stack application — frontend, backend, database, and auth — all generated together as a coherent, connected codebase. Watch your app stream into the editor in real time.

    When generation is complete, your app opens in a live preview. Click around, test features, and request changes directly in the chat. AIWA remembers the full context of what it built, so edits are safe and targeted — nothing breaks unexpectedly.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deploy to a Live URL">
    When you're ready to go live, click **Deploy**. Your app is instantly published to a `*.aiwacodes.com` subdomain — no build configuration, no server setup, no DNS management. AIWA handles the build pipeline, CDN, and SSL automatically.

    Share the URL right away. Your app is live.
  </Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
  Custom domains are available on paid plans. To connect your own domain (e.g., `yourapp.com`), upgrade to the Solo plan or above. AIWA handles SSL provisioning and CDN configuration automatically when you connect a custom domain. See [Plans & Pricing](/billing/plans) for details.
</Note>
