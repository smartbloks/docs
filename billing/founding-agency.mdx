## Why Only 25 Agencies

This cohort is intentionally small so the AIWA team can know every partner personally. Founding Partners receive dedicated onboarding calls, a private Slack channel with the team, and monthly strategy sessions that simply aren't possible at scale.

Once the 25 spots are filled, this level of access and pricing will never be offered again. The goal is to build a tight-knit group of agencies whose feedback directly shapes the future of AIWA — not to maximise sign-ups.
