## Plan Details

<Accordion title="Launchpad — Free">
  The Launchpad plan is free forever — no credit card required. You get **45 credits per month**, which resets on your billing cycle date each month. Launchpad includes:

  - Unlimited `*.aiwacodes.com` subdomains
  - Live preview + publish to a live URL
  - Remix any template + public share link
  - Version history + restore
  - AIWA badge on published projects

  Launchpad is ideal for solo builders validating ideas, learning the platform, or building personal projects before committing to a paid plan.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Solo — $25.99/mo (annual) / $29.99/mo (monthly)">
  Solo is billed at **$305/year** on the annual plan, or $29.99/month on the monthly plan. You get **130 credits per month** — roughly enough for two full apps. Solo includes everything in Launchpad, plus:

  - Remove the AIWA badge from your projects
  - Connect your own custom domains
  - Access the AIWA Max agent for more powerful generations
  - Export your source code or push directly to GitHub

  Solo is the right choice for freelancers and indie builders who are ready to deliver polished, badge-free projects under their own domain.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Agency — $149.99/mo (annual)">
  Agency is billed at **$1,565/year** on the annual plan. You get **790 credits per month** — roughly enough for 15 full apps. Agency includes everything in Solo, plus:

  - Invite clients to review and comment on projects
  - Export native Android APK and iOS App Store kits from any project
  - Up to 15 team seats with role-based access control
  - Visibility controls: set projects to public, unlisted, or private
  - VIP support with priority response times

  Agency is built for teams and studios delivering software at scale — multiple clients, multiple projects, and the collaboration tools to match.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Enterprise — Custom Pricing">
  Enterprise is for organisations that need more than the Agency plan can offer. Enterprise includes everything in Agency, plus:

  - Unlimited team seats and client accounts
  - SSO / SAML authentication and full audit log
  - Custom contracts and volume credit arrangements
  - Dedicated onboarding and account management

  To discuss Enterprise pricing and requirements, contact the AIWA team at [hello@aiwa.codes](mailto:hello@aiwa.codes).
</Accordion>

## Billing Details

- **Payments** are processed securely by Stripe
- **Annual plans** are billed upfront in a single charge; monthly plans are billed each calendar month
- **Upgrades** are prorated automatically — you only pay the difference for the remainder of your billing period
- **Cancellation** is available at any time — your projects remain accessible after cancellation until your paid period ends
- **Enterprise contracts** are available for volume discounts, custom SLAs, and tailored credit arrangements
