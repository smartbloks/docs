AIWA's SaaS Builder generates your entire SaaS product — including multi-tenant architecture, Stripe billing, onboarding flows, and a real database — then assigns AI agents to monitor MRR, reduce churn, and evolve the product based on what users actually do. You go from idea to a live, paying-customer-ready product without writing a line of code or hiring a single engineer.

## What Makes a SaaS Different

Building a SaaS is fundamentally different from building a website or a one-off app. SaaS products have unique requirements that general-purpose builders don't handle well: subscription billing with trial logic and upgrade flows, multi-tenant data isolation so one customer never sees another's records, onboarding sequences that convert free users into paying customers, and churn signals that need monitoring before they become revenue problems.

Most AI builders stop at "generate." They hand you a frontend and leave the hard parts — Stripe webhooks, row-level security, renewal logic — for you to figure out. AIWA handles all of these out of the box, because they're built into the generation process from the start.

## How to Build Your SaaS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a New Project">
    Log in to AIWA and start a new project. Choose **SaaS** as the builder type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe Your SaaS">
    Tell AIWA what your product does, who it's for, and how it makes money. Describe your pricing model, your core features, and the kind of users you're building for. No technical specs required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Product Manager Plans the Product">
    Before any code is written, your AI Product Manager defines the data model, user roles, core features, and subscription tiers. You review the plan and approve it — then building begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Full Stack Generated and Deployed">
    Frontend, backend, database, authentication, and Stripe billing are all generated together, connected, and deployed to your custom domain in one click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Agents Take Over Operations">
    After launch, your AI agent team monitors the product continuously. The CRO Agent watches your onboarding flow and trial conversion. The Revenue Agent tracks MRR signals. The Testing Agent validates every new feature before it ships. You approve changes — you don't manage code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's Included

**Invisible Managed Backend**
Every SaaS gets a dedicated Postgres database with row-level security, authentication, and real-time subscriptions — all provisioned automatically with zero configuration. Your tenants' data is isolated from day one.

**Stripe Payments Built In**
Tell AIWA your pricing model and it generates your subscription tiers, trial period logic, upgrade and downgrade flows, and Stripe webhook handling — all production-ready. You don't configure Stripe separately. It's part of your app.

**Multi-Model Code Generation**
Choose the AI model that fits your task: Claude Opus for complex business logic, Claude Sonnet for fast iterations, or GPT-4o as a capable alternative. Credits adjust based on model and complexity, and you can switch any time.

**Persistent App Context**
AIWA remembers every architectural decision, data model change, and feature addition across your entire project. Add a new module six months after launch and nothing breaks — the AI still has full context of what it built.

**One-Click Deploy + Custom Domain**
Ship your SaaS to a live URL directly from the editor. Connect your own domain and AIWA handles the build pipeline, SSL, and CDN automatically.

**Mobile App Export**
Turn your SaaS into a native mobile app with one click. Export a signed APK for Android or IPA for iOS, ready for app store submission — no second codebase required.
