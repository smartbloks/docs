AIWA's CRM Builder creates a fully working CRM designed around your sales process — not a generic template you have to work around. Describe how your team sells and AIWA builds the pipeline stages, contact fields, automations, and reports that match. No configuration screens, no weeks-long onboarding, and no paying for features your team will never touch.

## The Problem With Off-the-Shelf CRMs

Salesforce is too complex — it takes a certified administrator and months of setup before a sales rep can log a call. HubSpot is too expensive — by the time you unlock the features that actually matter, you're paying enterprise prices for a 5-person team. Notion databases don't scale — what starts as a clever pipeline template collapses the moment your deal volume grows or you need real automations.

The result is the same in every case: your team ignores the CRM. Deals live in spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads. You have no visibility into your pipeline, no reliable forecast, and no record of why deals were won or lost. The answer isn't a better off-the-shelf CRM. It's a CRM built for exactly how you sell.

## How to Build Your CRM

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a New Project">
    Log in to AIWA and start a new project. Choose **CRM** as the builder type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe Your Sales Process">
    Tell AIWA how leads come in, how deals progress through your pipeline, and what your team needs to track at each stage. Plain English — no forms, no config screens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Product Manager Maps the Structure">
    Your AI Product Manager designs the data model, pipeline structure, user roles, and automation logic before building begins. Review the plan and approve, or ask for changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your CRM Is Built and Deployed">
    A fully working CRM is generated and deployed with a live database from day one — contacts, deals, pipeline, reports, and automations, all accessible from any browser immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evolve as You Grow">
    Add pipeline stages, build new modules, or integrate your email and calendar tools as your process matures. AIWA keeps full context of your CRM so changes never break what's already working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's Included

**Custom Pipeline Builder**
Define your own deal stages, stage-level probabilities, and required fields per stage. Your pipeline reflects your actual sales motion — not preset stages you have to rename and work around.

**Contact & Company Management**
Full contact and company records with custom fields, complete activity history, tags, and relationship mapping. Attach deals, notes, files, and tasks to any record so everything your team needs is in one place.

**Activity Tracking**
Log calls, emails, meetings, and notes directly in the CRM. Set reminders and follow-up tasks that surface at the right time, so nothing goes cold and no follow-up is missed.

**Automated Workflows**
Trigger automations based on deal stage changes, field updates, or inactivity thresholds. Move deals forward, assign tasks, and send notifications without manual chasing.

**Pipeline & Revenue Reports**
Custom dashboards showing pipeline value by stage, conversion rates, average deal size, and revenue forecasts — built to your metrics, not generic defaults that don't map to how you sell.

**Team Access & Permissions**
Role-based access for reps, managers, and admins. Each person sees exactly what they need and nothing they shouldn't — without a complex permissions configuration exercise.

**Mobile Access**
Your CRM works on any device through the browser. When you're ready for a dedicated mobile experience, export your CRM as a native app — signed APK for Android, IPA for iOS.
