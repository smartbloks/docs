## How to Build Your App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a New Project">
    Log in to AIWA and start a new project. Choose **App** as the builder type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe Your App">
    Tell AIWA who your app is for, what it does, and how users move through it. No wireframes, no technical specifications required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Plans the Architecture">
    Your AI Product Manager maps the data model, user flows, component structure, and integration requirements before a single line of code is written — and keeps that context for the lifetime of your product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Full-Stack Generation Streams in Real Time">
    Frontend, backend, database, auth, and integrations are all generated together, connected and coherent. Watch it stream into your editor as a complete file tree.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Testing Agent Validates Before Deploy">
    Before anything ships, the AI Testing Agent checks layout, authentication flows, mobile rendering, and logic errors automatically. You see a clean build summary before you publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy to Web or Export to Mobile">
    One-click deploy ships your app to a live URL or your custom domain. When you're ready for mobile, export a signed APK for the Play Store or an IPA for the App Store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Agents Run Your Product">
    After launch, your AI agent team takes over. The SEO Agent surfaces ranking opportunities, the CRO Agent watches conversion behaviour, and the Revenue Agent monitors growth signals. You receive a weekly brief and approve fixes before they ship.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's Included in Every App

**Invisible Managed Backend**
Every app gets a dedicated Postgres database, authentication system, and file storage — provisioned automatically the moment your app needs them. You never write a migration, configure a connection string, or set up a table. It just works.

**Built-In Integrations**
Stripe for payments, Resend for transactional email, and Cloudinary for media management are generated directly into your app when you need them. No separate integration setup, no API key configuration screens.

**Persistent Context (Project Brain™)**
AIWA remembers every architectural decision, data model rationale, and component relationship across your entire project. Come back six months later to add a feature and nothing breaks — because the AI still knows exactly what it built and why.

**One-Click Deploy**
Your app ships to a live URL or custom domain with a single click. AIWA manages the build pipeline, SSL certificate, and CDN automatically. There is no server to provision and no deployment script to write.

## Supported AI Models

AIWA gives you access to multiple leading models so you can choose the right tool for each task:

- **Claude Opus** — best for complex architecture and reasoning-heavy features
- **Claude Sonnet** — fast, capable, and ideal for iterative builds
- **GPT-4o** — a strong alternative for diverse generation tasks
- **Gemini 2.5 Pro** — excellent for multimodal and long-context work

Credits adjust based on the model you choose and the complexity of what you're building.
