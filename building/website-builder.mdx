## How to Build Your Website

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a New Project">
    Log in to AIWA and start a new project. Choose **Website** as the builder type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe Your Site">
    Tell AIWA about your business, goals, and target audience in plain English. No forms, no templates — just describe what you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Site Plan">
    AIWA's AI Product Manager presents your site plan before anything is built: page hierarchy, content strategy, and overall architecture. Review and approve, or ask for adjustments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AIWA Builds Every Page">
    Full site generation streams in real time — landing, about, pricing, blog, and legal pages, all with consistent design and copy throughout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview on Desktop and Mobile">
    Review your site in the live preview. The AI Testing Agent automatically checks responsiveness across device sizes and flags any layout issues before you publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy With One Click">
    Connect your custom domain or publish instantly to a free `*.aiwacodes.com` subdomain. AIWA handles SSL, CDN, and deployment — your site is live in seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Agents Take Over">
    After launch, your AI agent team gets to work. The SEO Agent monitors your keyword rankings and flags drops. The CRO Agent watches visitor behaviour and surfaces conversion issues. You receive a weekly brief and approve any changes before they go live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Features

**Full Site Generation From Prompt**
Describe your business once and AIWA generates a complete site — architecture, copy, design, and structure — all coherent and consistent. Not a template. Not a starter theme.

**Mobile-First by Default**
Every site AIWA builds is fully responsive. The AI Testing Agent verifies your mobile layout automatically before anything goes live, so you never launch a broken experience on smaller screens.

**Continuous SEO Monitoring**
Your AI SEO Agent tracks keyword rankings over time. When traffic drops, it identifies the cause — whether that's a missing meta tag, a schema issue, a content gap, or broken internal links — and implements the approved fix.

**CRO Monitoring**
The AI CRO Agent watches how real visitors move through your site. When conversion rates dip or users drop off unexpectedly, it flags the friction point and proposes concrete changes to fix it.

**Custom Domain + One-Click Deploy**
Connect your existing domain in seconds. AIWA handles the full deployment pipeline — SSL certificate provisioning, CDN configuration, and build management — so you never touch a server.

**Live Database Backend**
Need a contact form, a member portal, or dynamic content that updates in real time? AIWA provisions a dedicated database automatically when your site requires one. You never configure a connection string or set up a table.

**Multi-Model AI Generation**
Choose the AI model that fits your project: Claude Opus for deep reasoning, GPT-4o for speed and versatility, or Gemini 2.5 Pro for multimodal tasks. Credits adjust based on model and complexity.

<Note>
The AI SEO and CRO agents send you a weekly brief summarising what they've found and what they recommend. You review their suggestions and approve changes before anything is updated on your live site.
</Note>
